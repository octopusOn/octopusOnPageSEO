# Cookie policy

## Cookies we use

{{company.site}} uses only the cookies the service needs to work:

- the session cookie (that you are signed in);
- a temporary cookie used during two-step sign-in;
- the cookie of the "trust this browser" option;
- your language choice.

No analytics, advertising or third-party tracking cookies are used. Necessary cookies need no consent. You can delete them in your browser, but you will then have to sign in again.
