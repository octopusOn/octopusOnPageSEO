## 5. Right of withdrawal

Under Article 15(1)(ğ) of the Turkish Distance Contracts Regulation, the right of withdrawal does not apply to services performed instantly in electronic form. Because the credits are loaded onto your account right after payment, with your consent, you have no right of withdrawal. You confirm this separately at the payment step.
